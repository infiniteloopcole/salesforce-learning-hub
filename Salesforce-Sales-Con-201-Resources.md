# Salesforce Sales-Con-201 Exam Study Resources & Preparation Guide

This document aggregates key learning materials, hands-on study notes, and practical strategies for clearing the **Salesforce Sales Consultant (Sales-Con-201)** certification exam.

---

## 📌 Core Domain Coverage

To pass the Sales-Con-201 exam, candidates should focus heavily on the following configuration and strategy domains:

* **Sales Cloud Implementation**: Designing custom objects, lead life cycles, and sales paths.
* **Opportunity & Pipeline Management**: Configuring stages, probability mapping, and forecasting metrics.
* **Security & Access Models**: OWDs, role hierarchies, sharing rules, and territory management.
* **Analytics & Dashboards**: Building actionable business insights for sales managers and reps.

## 💡 Practical Preparation Tips

1. **Leverage Developer Orgs**: Always test complex lead-routing and opportunity-stage rules in a hands-on environment before test day.
2. **Prioritize Native Features**: Choose out-of-the-box Salesforce configurations over custom APEX solutions unless explicit business constraints dictate otherwise.
3. **Pacing**: Aim to spend no more than 1.5 minutes per question during the live exam session.
