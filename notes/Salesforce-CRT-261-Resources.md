# Salesforce Service Cloud Consultant Architectural Guide (CRT-261) 🎧

Key architectural strategies for implementing multi-channel customer service, SLA management, and Knowledge Base lifecycle.

---

## 🔀 Core Architecture Topics

1. **Omni-Channel Routing:** Queue-Based Routing vs. Skills-Based Routing for real-time case distribution.
2. **Entitlements & Milestones:** Managing Customer SLAs, First Contact Resolution (FCR), and escalation processes.
3. **Salesforce Knowledge:** Article lifecycle management, KCS (Knowledge-Centered Service), and Experience Cloud integration.
